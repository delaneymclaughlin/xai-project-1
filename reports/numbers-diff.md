## Model quality and checks (seed 0)

- Hyperparameters chosen by 5-fold CV on realized value: depth 2, min_child_weight 300, 218 trees (ablation: depth 2, 214 trees).
- Test R^2 vs realized engagement value 0.029 (RMSE 0.844). The noise ceiling (R^2 of the TRUE expectation against realized value) is only 0.031, because single impressions are mostly zero with rare 5-point replies.
- Test R^2 vs the true expected value 0.884 (RMSE 0.0614); ablation 0.865.
- SHAP additivity max error 2.7e-07; TreeSHAP vs brute-force 2^7-coalition Shapley max difference 1.8e-07.
- Proxy effect bootstrap (1,000 resamples of held-out impressions): top decile 40.2-50.5%, ablated 38.0-47.5%. Ranking by the TRUE expected value gives 35.8%. This bootstrap resamples only the test rows of one fixed pair of models, so it understates uncertainty; the across-seed figures (which retrain) are the better guide.
- Across seeds, top-10% share minus ablated top-10% share: per seed +2.0, +3.5, +0.5, -1.0, -0.5 pp; mean +0.9, t-95% interval -1.4 to +3.2 pp. Overall distress share of impressions per seed: 14.0, 15.9, 14.8, 16.8, 15.5%.
- Faithfulness baseline: mean drop over the 20 other feature pairs (the top-2 pair is excluded).
- Support of the teen's posts in the training data (seed 0; training rows within 0.5 / 1 SD over the 5 continuous features): Post A 0 / 0; Post B 10 / 180; Post C 30 / 510; Post D 2 / 103; Post E 3 / 96; Post F 2 / 87; Post G 12 / 264; Post H 4 / 248.
